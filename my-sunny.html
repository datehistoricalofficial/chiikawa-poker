A Chance Encounter

On a flight somewhere between take-off and landing, he noticed her. Not just noticed — he remembered. In a world where people scroll past each other endlessly, he paused.

November 1, 2025. The first message: "Nice to meet you Becka. 還是Becca?" Three dots. A reply. The beginning of something neither of them expected.

He said he only pays attention to interesting people. She turned out to be far more than interesting — she became the happiest thing about his entire year.
